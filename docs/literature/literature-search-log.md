# Literature Search and Citation Verification Log

Search date: 2026-07-27

## Scope

The search was intentionally focused on primary sources needed to position the
diagnostic Pilot: lexical and neural retrieval, multi-stage reranking,
adaptive retrieval, hierarchical context, detrimental retrieved evidence,
calibration and selective prediction, and legal information retrieval. The
manuscript does not claim a systematic review.

## Sources searched

- ACL Anthology for retrieval, reranking, adaptive RAG, RAG evaluation, and
  noisy-context studies.
- PMLR for probability calibration and selective prediction.
- NeurIPS/OpenReview for RAG, BEIR, Self-RAG, and hierarchical retrieval.
- Publisher pages and DOI records for BM25, COLIEE, annotation agreement, and
  paired tests.

## Verification procedure

Each retained reference was checked against its primary proceedings or
publisher page. DOI-bearing references were also checked against DOI/Crossref
metadata when available. OpenReview papers were retained only when the page
identified the work as an accepted conference paper. Claims in the manuscript
were limited to the abstracts, method descriptions, or results visible in the
primary source.

## Retained themes and representative sources

1. Sparse and neural retrieval: Robertson and Zaragoza (2009), Karpukhin et
   al. (2020), Thakur et al. (2021).
2. Multi-stage reranking: Nogueira et al. (2020).
3. Adaptive retrieval: Jiang et al. (2023), Asai et al. (2024), Jeong et al.
   (2024).
4. Hierarchical context: Sarthi et al. (2024).
5. Retrieval noise and risk: Oh and Thorne (2023), Hong et al. (2024), Thakur
   et al. (2024).
6. RAG evaluation: Es et al. (2024), Saad-Falcon et al. (2024).
7. Calibration and abstention: Guo et al. (2017), Geifman and El-Yaniv (2019).
8. Legal retrieval and cross-domain legal NLP: Chalkidis et al. (2021, 2022)
   and Goebel et al. (2024).

## Exclusions

Survey articles, vendor blog posts, unreviewed architecture lists, and papers
whose contribution was not used in the manuscript argument were excluded.
Recent work after 2024 was not necessary for the study claim and was omitted
to keep the bibliography proportionate to the target journal.
