# Manuscript Submission-Polish Design

## Scope

Apply a targeted pre-submission revision to the diagnostic manuscript without
adding data, models, paths, or experiments.

## Approved changes

1. Identify Kaifeng Sun as the sole author and add the known affiliation,
   China Jiliang University, China.
2. Replace the dense abstract with a shorter result-led version and update the
   keywords.
3. Add one compact table that makes the two-domain differences explicit.
4. Keep a concise NO-GO result in the main text and move its detailed criteria
   to an appendix.
5. Add schematic, paraphrased examples of HARMFUL evidence to the appendix.
6. Tighten the conclusion and ethics wording, add a sole-author contribution
   statement, and remove the potentially identifying “earlier manuscripts”
   wording.
7. Add unobtrusive arrowheads to both schematic figures so that every line has
   an explicit direction.

## Boundaries

Unknown department, email, ORCID, funding, conflict-of-interest, and
acknowledgement information will not be invented. The revision will not expose
question-level private records or copyrighted source passages. A separate
revised DOCX will be generated because the prior DOCX may be open in Word.

## Verification

suite and document audits, render every DOCX page to PNG, and visually inspect
all pages before delivery.
